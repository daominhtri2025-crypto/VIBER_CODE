## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. Describe a place you enjoy visiting.** *(Tả một nơi bạn thích đến thăm.)*

> One place I enjoy visiting is Đà Lạt. I go there about once a year with my family. I love the cool weather, the flowers, and the quiet streets. I like walking around the lake and visiting the night markets. Đà Lạt makes me feel relaxed and happy.

**Q2. Describe a place where you like to relax.** *(Tả một nơi bạn thích thư giãn.)*

> A place where I like to relax is a small coffee shop near my house. It is quiet, has soft music, and a nice view of trees. I usually go there on weekends to read a book or just sit and enjoy the calm atmosphere. It is my favorite place to take a break from work.

**Q3. Would you rather live in a city center, a suburb, or a rural area?** *(Bạn muốn sống ở trung tâm, ngoại ô hay nông thôn?)*

> I would rather live in a suburb. It is quieter than the city center but still close to shops, schools, and hospitals. The city center is often noisy and crowded, while rural areas can be hard to find good services and jobs. The suburb gives me both comfort and peace.

**Q4. What kinds of tourism are most common in your country?** *(Loại hình du lịch nào phổ biến nhất ở nước bạn?)*

> In Vietnam, cultural and nature tourism are the most common. Many people visit Hạ Long Bay, Hội An, Đà Lạt, and Sa Pa. Beach tourism is also popular in Phú Quốc and Nha Trang. Tourists enjoy the scenery, local food, and traditional festivals.

**Q5. What form of tourism do you prefer, and why?** *(Bạn thích loại hình du lịch nào? Vì sao?)*

> I prefer cultural tourism because I enjoy learning about history, food, and traditions. When I visit temples, museums, or old towns, I understand the country better. I also like talking to local people, which makes the trip more meaningful.

**Q6. What are the benefits of eco-tourism?** *(Lợi ích của du lịch sinh thái là gì?)*

> Eco-tourism helps protect nature, because tourists learn to respect forests and wildlife. It also creates jobs for local people, such as guides, cooks, or sellers of handmade products. In short, it is good for both the environment and the local community.
