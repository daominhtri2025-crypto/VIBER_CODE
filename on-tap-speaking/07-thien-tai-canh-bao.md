## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. What kinds of natural disasters occur in your country?** *(Những loại thiên tai nào xảy ra ở đất nước bạn?)*

> In Vietnam, the common natural disasters are floods, storms, and landslides. Floods often happen in the central region during the rainy season. Storms and typhoons bring strong winds and heavy rain from July to October. Landslides can occur in the mountains after heavy rain.

**Q2. How do officials in your country alert people when a natural hazard might occur?** *(Chính quyền nước bạn cảnh báo người dân thế nào khi có thể xảy ra thiên tai?)*

> Officials use many ways to alert people. They broadcast warnings on TV, radio, and street loudspeakers. They also send text messages to phones and post updates on social media like Facebook and Zalo. In serious cases, local officials or police go directly to warn people.

**Q3. Have you heard about storms in the news recently? What impacts did they have?** *(Gần đây bạn có nghe về các cơn bão trên tin tức không? Chúng gây tác động gì?)*

> Yes, I heard about two storms in Vietnam this year. Storm Wutip caused floods in central Vietnam and cut off power. Storm Wipha brought heavy rain and landslides to the north. These storms showed how dangerous natural disasters can be.

> **Lưu ý:** Trong bài nói, nên dùng *typhoon* (bão) thay cho *hurricane*. Kiểm tra lại tên bão và thời điểm trước khi thi.
