## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. What are some benefits and drawbacks of living in an extended family?** *(Sống trong gia đình nhiều thế hệ có lợi ích và bất lợi gì?)*

> Living in an extended family has both good and bad sides. One benefit is support: grandparents can help take care of children. But one drawback is less privacy. It can be noisy, and people may have different opinions, which can cause arguments.

**Q2. Describe a person who has influenced you.** *(Tả một người đã ảnh hưởng đến bạn.)*

> One person who has influenced me is my mother. She is kind, hardworking, and always supports me. She taught me to be polite, responsible, and never give up. When I feel stressed, she gives me good advice. She is my biggest role model.

**Q3. Describe a friend who is important to you.** *(Tả một người bạn quan trọng với bạn.)*

> One friend who is important to me is Thương, my best friend from high school. She is friendly and funny, and she always helps me when I feel stressed or sad. We share stories and support each other in study and life. Thương is like a sister to me.

**Q4. Describe a piece of advice someone gave you.** *(Tả một lời khuyên ai đó đã dành cho bạn.)*

> One piece of advice I remember is from my teacher: "Don't be afraid to make mistakes when learning English." At that time I was shy. Her advice gave me confidence, so I started speaking more in class. Now I know that mistakes are part of learning.
