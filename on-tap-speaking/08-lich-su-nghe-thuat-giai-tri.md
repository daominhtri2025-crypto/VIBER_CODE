## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. What questions about the past do you think archaeologists are trying to answer?** *(Nhà khảo cổ đang cố trả lời những câu hỏi gì về quá khứ?)*

> Archaeologists want to know how ancient people lived, including what they ate, where they lived, and what tools they used. They also ask why some old cities disappeared, whether because of war, weather, or disease. Finally, they study people's beliefs and traditions.

**Q2. Why is it important to study the past?** *(Vì sao nghiên cứu quá khứ lại quan trọng?)*

> Studying the past helps us understand how people lived and solved problems. We can learn from past mistakes, such as wars or pollution, to make better choices today. It also shows where we come from and helps us build a better future.

**Q3. Describe a movie or TV show you enjoy.** *(Tả một bộ phim hoặc chương trình truyền hình bạn thích.)*

> One TV show I enjoy is *MasterChef Vietnam*. It is a cooking competition where judges taste the dishes and choose who goes to the next round. I love food and cooking, so I enjoy seeing how creative the contestants are. I also learn new recipes from the show.

**Q4. Describe a song that means a lot to you.** *(Tả một bài hát có ý nghĩa đặc biệt với bạn.)*

> One song that means a lot to me is "Lạc trôi" by Sơn Tùng M-TP. I listened to it often in high school. The melody is unique and the song feels emotional. It helped me relax during stressful times and reminds me of my friends.

**Q5. Describe a piece of artwork you created.** *(Tả một tác phẩm nghệ thuật bạn đã tạo ra.)*

> One piece of artwork I created is a watercolor painting of a sunset. I painted it during my summer holiday. It shows the sun going down behind mountains, with orange and pink colors. It was not perfect, but I was proud of it, and I still keep it in my room.

**Q6. Describe a favorite book from your childhood.** *(Tả một cuốn sách yêu thích thời thơ ấu.)*

> One of my favorite childhood books is *Doraemon*. It is a Japanese comic about a robot cat who helps a boy named Nobita with his futuristic gadgets. I read it almost every week. It made me laugh and taught me lessons about friendship and kindness.
