## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. Describe a memorable meal you had.** *(Tả một bữa ăn đáng nhớ.)*

> One memorable meal was my birthday dinner last year. My family took me to a small restaurant near our house. We ate hotpot and seafood. The atmosphere was warm, and we laughed and shared stories. It was not a big party, but I will always remember it.

**Q2. Describe a memorable trip you took.** *(Tả một chuyến đi đáng nhớ.)*

> One memorable trip was to Đà Nẵng with my friends during summer break. We visited the beach, Bà Nà Hills, and the Dragon Bridge. The weather was nice, and the food was delicious. We took many photos and made great memories.

**Q3. Describe a special event you attended.** *(Tả một sự kiện đặc biệt bạn đã tham dự.)*

> One special event I attended was my university graduation ceremony. It was held in the school hall. I wore a gown and hat and took photos with my friends and teachers. My family came to support me, which made the day even more special.

**Q4. Describe a challenging experience you faced.** *(Tả một trải nghiệm khó khăn bạn từng trải qua.)*

> One challenging experience was moving to a new school in grade 10. At first I did not know anyone and felt shy. The lessons were also harder. After some time, I made new friends and asked teachers for help. It taught me to be brave and adapt to new things.

**Q5. Describe a memorable conversation you had.** *(Tả một cuộc trò chuyện đáng nhớ.)*

> One memorable conversation was with my teacher before the final exam. I was nervous, and she said kindly, "Believe in yourself. You have worked hard." Her words made me feel calm and strong. After that, I did well in the exam.

**Q6. Describe a gift you have received.** *(Tả một món quà bạn đã nhận được.)*

> One special gift was a watch from my parents on my birthday last year. It has a black strap and a silver face, and I wear it almost every day. It shows their love and support, and it helps me manage my time better.

**Q7. Describe a family tradition you participate in.** *(Tả một truyền thống gia đình bạn tham gia.)*

> I always take part in celebrating Tết. My family cleans the house, decorates with flowers, and prepares bánh chưng and spring rolls. We wear new clothes, visit relatives, and give lucky money in red envelopes. It is a happy time when the whole family gets together.

**Q8. Describe a pet you have or had.** *(Tả một con vật cưng bạn đang nuôi hoặc đã từng nuôi.)*

> I used to have a white cat named Mimi. I got her in high school. Every day she waited for me at the door and followed me around the house. She made me feel relaxed after school, and I still miss her.
