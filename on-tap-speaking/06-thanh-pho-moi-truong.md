## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. What are two ways to enhance the quality of life in cities?** *(Hai cách nâng cao chất lượng sống ở thành phố là gì?)*

> I think two important ways are better public transportation and more green spaces. Good buses and trains reduce traffic and air pollution. Parks and trees make cities more relaxing and healthy, and they help people reduce stress.

**Q2. What can you do to reduce energy consumption?** *(Bạn có thể làm gì để giảm tiêu thụ năng lượng?)*

> First, I turn off lights, fans, and electronics when I do not use them. Second, I use energy-saving devices like LED lights. I also walk or ride a bicycle for short trips instead of using a car. These small actions save electricity and protect the environment.

**Q3. What aspects of city life appeal to you? What do you not like?** *(Điều gì ở cuộc sống thành phố hấp dẫn bạn? Điều gì bạn không thích?)*

> I like the services, jobs, and entertainment in cities, such as hospitals, cinemas, and cafés. However, I do not like the noise, the traffic jams, and the dirty air. Living in the city can also be expensive.
