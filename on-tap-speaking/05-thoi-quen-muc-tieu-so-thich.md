## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. What are the two ways in which establishing a routine can make your life easier?** *(Thiết lập thói quen giúp cuộc sống dễ dàng hơn theo hai cách nào?)*

> Having a routine makes life easier in two ways. First, it saves time, because you do not have to think about what to do next. Second, it reduces stress, because you feel organized and your day is under control. So a good routine helps with both time and stress.

**Q2. Describe a goal you have set for yourself.** *(Tả một mục tiêu bạn đã đặt ra cho bản thân.)*

> One goal I have set is to improve my English speaking skills. I want to speak more fluently and confidently. I practice every day by watching English videos and speaking with friends. I also try to learn five new words each day. Sometimes it is hard, but I do not give up.

**Q3. Describe a hobby you enjoy.** *(Tả một sở thích bạn yêu thích.)*

> One hobby I enjoy is drawing. I started when I was a child, and I usually draw on weekends. I like to draw people, nature, and cartoons. Drawing helps me relax and express my feelings. It is a simple hobby, but it brings me a lot of joy.

**Q4. Describe your favorite type of exercise.** *(Tả loại hình thể dục bạn yêu thích.)*

> My favorite exercise is jogging. I jog in the park near my house three or four times a week. It helps me stay healthy and clear my mind after a long day. It is easy to do and does not need special equipment.

**Q5. Describe a recipe you have tried.** *(Tả một công thức nấu ăn bạn đã thử.)*

> One recipe I have tried is fried rice. I learned it from my mom. I fried cooked rice with eggs, vegetables, and a little soy sauce. Sometimes I add chicken or sausage. It is simple and quick, and now I often cook it when I am busy or hungry.
