## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. Describe an object or invention that you use regularly.** *(Tả một vật dụng bạn dùng thường xuyên.)*

> My smartphone is the object I use most. I use it every day to check messages, read the news, and study online. It is very convenient because I can do many things with one device. It saves time and helps me stay connected with my family.

**Q2. Describe a gadget you would like to own.** *(Tả một thiết bị bạn muốn sở hữu.)*

> I would like to own a tablet. It is lighter than a laptop but bigger than a phone, so it is easy to carry. I want to use it for reading and studying online, and with a smart pen I could take notes. I think it would help me study more effectively.

**Q3. What app or website do you use regularly?** *(Bạn thường dùng ứng dụng hoặc trang web nào?)*

> I use ChatGPT regularly. I use it to practice English, get writing ideas, and answer my grammar questions. It explains things clearly and quickly, so it saves me a lot of time.

**Q4. Describe a piece of furniture in your home.** *(Tả một món nội thất trong nhà.)*

> My study desk is my favorite piece of furniture. It is made of wood and has drawers for my books. I use it every day for studying and working on my laptop. It is near the window, so I have fresh air and natural light. It helps me stay organized and focused.
