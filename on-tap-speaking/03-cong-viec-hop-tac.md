## 2. Câu hỏi gợi ý và trả lời ngắn

**Q1. In what ways do employees cooperate in a workplace? Give two examples.** *(Nhân viên hợp tác với nhau thế nào? Đưa ra hai ví dụ.)*

> Employees cooperate by working in teams and helping each other. First, in a team project, each person does a different part and shares ideas. Second, when someone has a problem, others can explain or give support. This builds a good team spirit.

**Q2. How would you react to an aggressive coworker?** *(Bạn sẽ phản ứng thế nào với đồng nghiệp hung hăng?)*

> I would try to stay calm and not argue. I think it is better to listen first and talk later when things are quiet. For example, once a coworker shouted at me about a mistake. I did not shout back. After the meeting, I spoke to him politely, and we solved the problem together.

**Q3. Describe a project you worked on.** *(Tả một dự án bạn đã tham gia.)*

> One project I worked on was a group presentation about the environment. I worked with three classmates. I helped design the slides and presented part of the talk. We practiced together after class. At first I was nervous, but our teacher said it was clear and creative. It improved my teamwork and speaking skills.

**Q4. What skills do police detectives need? Why?** *(Thám tử cần những kỹ năng gì? Vì sao?)*

> Detectives need good observation skills to find small clues at a crime scene. They also need problem-solving skills to connect the facts and find the truth. Communication is important, because they must ask questions and talk to people. They also need patience, since some cases take a long time.
